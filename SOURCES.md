# Sources and reuse terms

Last checked: 2026-11-24. Re-check before each content update.

## How content is used

- **ontario.ca:** materials may be reproduced for **non-commercial** purposes if credit is given, Crown copyright is acknowledged, and **no changes are made** to the original content ([copyright page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/copyright-information)). CareMap therefore does **not** copy or edit ontario.ca text. It stores short summaries written in its own words, and every answer links to the original page.
  © King's Printer for Ontario.
- **Ontario Health (ontariohealth.ca):** requires prior written permission for reproduction beyond personal use. CareMap **links only** and does not use its content.
- **Association of Ontario Midwives and 211 Ontario:** CareMap states facts from these pages in its own words (no copied text) and links to the originals.
- **Hospital "Know your options" handouts:** not used; terms vary by hospital.

## Emergency signs (safety rules)

The red-flag list in `src/lib/safety.ts` follows the "call 911" signs on the [Emergency rooms](https://www.ontario.ca/page/emergency-rooms) page: loss of consciousness, pain that pain medication doesn't relieve, confusion, persistent severe chest pain, difficulty breathing.
